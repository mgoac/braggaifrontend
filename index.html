CH.05 — FAQ

The four questions
about the bot

Is this the official FAMU bot?+

No. BraggAI is an unofficial, student-made resource project. It is not operated by Florida A&M University and is not endorsed by the university. Think of it as a really well-read hall mate.

Is my identity kept private?+

Sessions are anonymous. We do not collect student IDs, emails, or history that ties a question to a person. We keep aggregate counts — like “4120 answers on file” — and nothing more.

How current is the information?+

Every answer carries a source and a date-stamp. Sources are cross-checked against official FAMU pages and staff-verified bulletins, then refreshed weekly against the academic calendar. Semester changes — dining hours, shuttle runs, game-day roads — get priority re-checks.

I found a wrong answer. What do I do?+

Flag it. There’s a correction slip on every answer card. Each flag is read by a human, week-of. The title for the semester belongs to whoever files the best correction.
